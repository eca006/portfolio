OVERVIEW PAGE · VISUALIZATIONS
SIMULATED TRADING FLOW

Helping users see what they’ve spent—and what they’ve gained or lost.

Working with a team, I created the overview page and visualizations for a stock-market investment tracking app. My work brought spending and investment gains and losses into a single view.

I also built the prototype’s stock-trading flow. The app used stock price data from Yahoo Finance, while trading remained simulated; it did not execute real trades.

Explore my contribution

Overview and visualizations

I designed and built the overview page to help users understand spending and investment performance, including gains and losses.

Trading interaction

I built the system for trading stocks within the prototype. This let us explore the trading experience without connecting to a live brokerage.

Project scope

A class team prototype with connected Yahoo Finance price data. It was not a production trading platform, and trading rules and safeguards were not fully enforced.
